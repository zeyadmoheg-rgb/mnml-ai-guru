# Three Indoor Set-Design Concepts — Egyptian Magic, Wonder Register
### El Behiry × Mardini · All fully indoor, story-led, no horror
Each concept lists verified Vimeo references (all links checked live).

---

## IDEA 1 — "HEKAYAT TETA" (My Grandmother's Tales)
One warm lamplit room whose walls open like theater flats into three myth sets.

- **Set:** grandmother's balcony room (lamp-gold, radio, tea, henna) with wild
  walls; behind each wall a tale set — the golden Nile (shallow tank, lotus),
  the full-moon rooftop (practical moon lamp, laundry lines), the protection
  room (bakhour smoke, mirrors, blue beads and khamsa as jewelry story).
- **Story:** Teta tells; the model steps into each tale, dressed for it; at the
  end she becomes the teller to a child. Transmission of Egyptian stories —
  Mardini's thesis, literalized.
- **Vimeo refs:**
  - Wes Anderson — "Castello Cavalcanti" (Prada): https://vimeo.com/80059810
    · a whole village on a Cinecittà soundstage; warm theatrical storytelling —
    the set logic and tone.
  - Mardini — Kotn "Karyetna": https://vimeo.com/1161750576 · his own warmest
    film (grandfather + child); proof this register lives inside him.
  - Oren Lavie — "Her Morning Elegance": https://vimeo.com/13781225 · magic
    done entirely in-camera on one small set; the trick-craft spirit for the
    tale transitions (flats, props, no VFX).

## IDEA 2 — "AROUSET EL MOULID" (The Sugar Bride)
The iconic moulid sugar-doll bride comes to life overnight in the
sweet-maker's workshop.

- **Set:** an old halawa workshop after closing — copper pots, pink and white
  sugar dust, shelves of arousa dolls in their paper-fan dresses, one bare
  bulb swinging to moonlight through the shutter. At midnight the largest
  arousa wakes: the workshop becomes her palace — paper fans unfold into
  architecture, tinsel becomes chandeliers, sugar dust becomes snow-glitter.
- **Story:** one night of freedom — she explores, dances, tries on the
  sweet-maker's world; at dawn she returns to her shelf, but leaves one paper
  fan open on his bench. He smiles: he knows. Wonder, humor, tenderness.
- **Why it fits:** the arousa is a beloved pure-Egyptian design object (moulid
  culture — Mardini's own documented territory, flipped from trance to
  sweetness); her costume IS couture (structured paper-fan dress = a fashion
  designer's dream brief); Behiry's KOJAK pink-drapery register is literally
  this palette. Most original of the three — no one has done it seriously.
- **Vimeo refs:**
  - Sevdaliza — "Shahmaran" (dir. Emmanuel Adjei): https://vimeo.com/283664332
    · a myth-figure staged sumptuously inside a built world; myth as luxury.
  - Wes Anderson — "Come Together" (H&M): https://vimeo.com/224473293 · a
    complete pastel world inside one built interior; controlled color joy.
  - Mardini — "Ballet du Caire": https://vimeo.com/1205256965 · his slow-motion
    movement language for her dance sequences.

## IDEA 3 — "LAYALI EL BADR" (Full-Moon Nights)
A Cairo rooftop built entirely on stage under a huge practical moon —
the night when wishes are spoken to the moon.

- **Set:** one rooftop in forced perspective — laundry lines, satellite
  dishes, pigeon tower, distant painted skyline with lit windows; a 4–6m
  practical moon lamp on a crane that can descend close enough to touch.
- **Story:** wishes travel — a girl whispers a wish to the moon; the moon
  carries it (light shifting rooftop to rooftop) past neighbors' small
  scenes (an old man's radio, kids asleep among pigeons, a bride-to-be) and
  brings an answer back by dawn. Interconnection — one sky over everyone.
- **Why it fits:** the built-exterior-indoors is a proud theatrical choice
  (the moon as a lamp is the poster image); pure wonder, zero fear; rooftop
  culture is deeply Cairene and Mardini's own turf shot from a new angle.
- **Vimeo refs:**
  - Mardini — "A Portrait of Cairo": https://vimeo.com/1162810878 · the real
    rooftops and dusk light the set must honor.
  - Kahlil Joseph — "Until the Quiet Comes": https://vimeo.com/48551671 ·
    camera drifting through a community at night (language only, not tone).
  - Mardini — Kotn "Tefnut": https://vimeo.com/1161739130 · dusk-into-night
    palette with one practical light source in a vast dark space.

---

## Steer
**AROUSET EL MOULID is the boldest and most ownable** — a pure-Egyptian icon,
never done seriously, indoor by nature, couture built into the premise, and it
lets Mardini revisit moulid culture (his documented territory) in the wonder
register. **HEKAYAT TETA** is the safest and most campaign-flexible (three
looks, three palettes, anthology cuts). **LAYALI EL BADR** has the strongest
single poster image (the touchable moon) but the thinnest narrative of the
three — pick it only if the film is short-format.
