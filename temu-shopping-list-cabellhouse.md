product URLs are session/region-bound and die when shared, so per-item links
into Temu search are the stable way to buy).

Bambu Lab, Divoom, FlexiSpot, Snapmaker, Vestaboard, Leesa, Sihoo, Hbada,
xGIMI, QCY, ESR, Homedics...) is NOT sold genuinely on Temu. Links for those
point to Temu's generic equivalent of the same product type. Duplicated links
