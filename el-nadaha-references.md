# EL NADAHA — Reference Guide
Companion to `project-concepts-myth-magic.md`. Image pack delivered to the
creative director (14 frames + moodboard sheet). What each block is FOR:

## A — *Atlantics* (Mati Diop, 2019 · DP Claire Mathon)
The grade and the supernatural logic.
- **A1 dusk sea:** the master palette — Nile blue-black water, silver-grey sky,
  no gloss. This is the grade target for the whole film.
- **A2 night village palms:** Act 1 exterior mood — village holding its breath.
- **A3 possessed white eyes:** how the supernatural reads on a real face with
  zero VFX — makeup, contact lenses, stillness. The call's effect on the man.
- **A4 night street fire:** the protection-ritual warmth (henna-orange accent)
  against the blue night.

## B — *The Tragedy of Macbeth* (Joel Coen, 2021 · DP Bruno Delbonnel)
Proof that a myth shot entirely on a soundstage gains power from abstraction.
- **B1 torch in reeds:** almost a literal El Nadaha frame — a lone flame moving
  through a built reed field at night. Act 2 key reference.
- **B2 figures in fog void:** the walk — fog as set design, actors dissolving
  into white/black nothing.
- **B3 graphic shadow architecture:** the bridge to Behiry — hard single-source
  light carving geometry; his souk shadow language, monumentalized.
- **B4 stage tableau arches:** hero-tableau staging — frontal, symmetrical,
  theatrical but severe.

## C — *Under the Skin* (Jonathan Glazer, 2013 · DP Daniel Landin)
The meeting — Act 3's black-void seduction space.
- **C1 figure in black void:** the man standing in her space; floor as mirror.
- **C2 floating body:** the underwater/suspension feel without water.
- **C3 gold transformation:** the texture of being changed — abstract, tactile,
  no creature effects.

## D — Internal DNA (the two artists' own work)
- **D1 Mardini Nile dusk (Nubia):** the real-world anchor — the film must feel
  continuous with this frame or the myth loses its home.
- **D2 Behiry hard light / graphic shadow (Vogue souk):** his light discipline,
  to be transposed from sun to moon.
- **D3 Mardini curtained interior (GQ MENA):** the ritual room — Act 1's
  amulet/bakhour scene texture: curtained light, vintage domestic dread.

## Named refs without images (look them up in prep)
- **Gregory Crewdson — "Beneath the Roses" era:** night suburbia built on
  soundstages with fog and cinema lighting; the production methodology for our
  village-edge set. (His recent daylight work is the wrong register — pull the
  2003–2008 night tableaux specifically.)
- **Paolo Roversi:** grain, long-exposure ghosting, couture as apparition —
  El Nadaha's stills register.
- **Youssef Nabil:** hand-tinted Egyptian night glamour — the historical
  Egyptian precedent for staged myth without orientalism.
- **Shadi Abdel Salam — *Al-Mummia / The Night of Counting the Years* (1969):**
  the sacred-slow Egyptian cadence; the dignity benchmark every Egyptian
  crew member will recognize.
